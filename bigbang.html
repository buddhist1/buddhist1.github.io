Buddha's modern scientific description of the death of universe

Currently, there are several scientific theories regarding the end of the universe. The most popular one is big freeze / heat death.

Big Freeze or Heat Death the universe will eventually run out of usable energy as it continues to expand forever. Imagine the universe as a giant machine that slowly cools down; as galaxies drift further apart, stars eventually burn through their fuel and go dark, leaving behind only black holes that also eventually evaporate. Over trillions of years, the temperature everywhere will drop to nearly absolute zero, and matter will become so thin and cold that no new stars can form and no energy can be exchanged. In this final state, the universe becomes a vast, dark, and silent void where nothing ever happens again because the energy required for movement or life is perfectly and permanently spread out.

For a long time, scientists believed that the Big freeze is the most likely scenario due to the accelerating expansion of the universe which assumed that dark energy is a fixed, unchanging density of space. However, by creating the most detailed 3D map of the cosmos to date—using over 6 million galaxies to trace the universe's growth—DESI researchers found that dark energy appears to be weakening over cosmic time (source). This suggests that instead of it being a static property of the vacuum, dark energy might be a dynamic field that fluctuates. The data specifically points to a "sputtering" effect, where the acceleration of the universe's expansion was stronger in the distant past than it is today.

This discovery significantly shifts our understanding of how the universe might end. If dark energy continues to weaken or eventually decays to zero, the current era of accelerated expansion could plateau. At some point, the universe would stop expanding and begin to contract. This would lead to the "Big Crunch," a violent collapse where all matter and radiation are compressed back into a hot, dense singularity, potentially mirroring the conditions of the Big Bang.

This research has been analyzed by thousands of astrophysicists and currently is sitting at 4.2σ, which means that the chance for it to be wrong / purely statistical fluke is 0.0033%. This research is scheduled to conclude in late 2026 / early 2027 when it achieves 5σ (chance of it being statistical fluke is 1 in 3.5 million). If this research is concluded to be true, this might hint that the universe is a cyclic process of expansion and contraction.

Kappasutta

“Mendicants, an eon contains four uncountable periods. What four? When an eon contracts, it’s not easy to calculate how many years, how many hundreds or thousands or hundreds of thousands of years it takes. When an eon remains fully contracted, it’s not easy to calculate how many years, how many hundreds or thousands or hundreds of thousands of years it takes. When an eon expands, it’s not easy to calculate how many years, how many hundreds or thousands or hundreds of thousands of years it takes. When an eon remains fully expanded, it’s not easy to calculate how many years, how many hundreds or thousands or hundreds of thousands of years it takes. These are the four uncountable periods of an eon.”

Aggaññasutta

There comes a time when, Vāseṭṭha, after a very long period has passed, this cosmos contracts. As the cosmos contracts, sentient beings are mostly headed for the realm of streaming radiance. There they are mind-made, feeding on rapture, self-luminous, wandering in midair, steadily glorious, and they remain like that for a very long time.

There comes a time when, after a very long period has passed, this cosmos expands. As the cosmos expands, sentient beings mostly pass away from that host of radiant deities and come back to this realm. In the Brahmajālasutta, beings pass from one Brahmā realm to another, but when they come to this world it is as a human. Here we have a different perspective, where beings apparently still like the Brahmā gods exist in the physical realm. Here they are mind-made, feeding on rapture, self-luminous, wandering in midair, steadily glorious, and they remain like that for a very long time.

sutta source

The 2 suttas above are hinting that the universe follows a cyclic model of expansion and contraction, which currently has a very strong possibility to be proven to be true. Interestingly, ancient Buddhist pali text from thousand of years ago seems to have similar conclusion on how the universe form and ends
